Why the long way

The short answer is rarely the whole story.

Ask why Koreans take off their shoes indoors and you'll usually hear “to keep the floor clean.” True — but the long way goes further: through ondol heated floors, centuries of sitting and sleeping on the floor, and the quiet meaning of the entryway.

That's the road this blog takes. One custom at a time, researched properly and told in plain English, for travelers, expats and anyone curious about Korea.
