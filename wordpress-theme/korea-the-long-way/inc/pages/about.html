We believe that the best way to understand Korea is to look beyond the surface and discover the stories, traditions, and everyday life behind its culture.

WELCOME TO KOREA, THE LONG WAY is a place to explore Korean culture through everyday experiences, traditions, food, etiquette, K-POP, K-Drama, language, and more.

Born and raised in Korea and living in the United States for about 30 years, I have experienced both Korean and American cultures. Through this blog, I share Korean culture from a personal perspective and explain the little details that can make Korea easier to understand.

What You’ll Find Here

On WELCOME TO KOREA, THE LONG WAY, you’ll find articles about:

  • Korean culture, traditions, and etiquette
  • Korean food and dining culture
  • K-POP and Korean entertainment
  • K-Drama and Korean lifestyle
  • Korean holidays and celebrations
  • Korean language and everyday expressions
  • Everyday life in Korea
  • Korean and American cultural differences

Our Approach

Korean culture is more than what we see in K-POP, K-Drama, or travel videos.

We want to explore the stories and everyday customs behind Korean culture and explain them in a simple and approachable way.

Whether you are interested in visiting Korea, learning about Korean culture, enjoying K-POP and K-Drama, or simply curious about everyday life in Korea, we hope you find something interesting and useful here.
